Research Overview

This computational study evaluates structural variations within the SLG1/CTU2 gene of rice (Oryza sativa subsp. japonica). Utilizing digital computational science workflow modeling, this platform demonstrates the structural stability enhancements of mutant configurations compared to standard baseline baselines.
